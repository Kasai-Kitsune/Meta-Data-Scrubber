Currently supported

Images: PNG, JPG/JPEG, WebP, HEIC/HEIF → PNG

Documents: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, ODT, ODS, ODP

Not currently supported

DNG/RAW, GIF, animated WebP, video, audio, archives, and uncommon containers. Video and audio require ffmpeg, which is not bundled or fetched. Unsupported files are rejected rather than reported as clean.

How it works

Images (incl. HEIC): visible pixels are decoded and written to a new image. HEIC is converted to PNG (preserves transparency). EXIF and similar metadata are not intentionally copied.

Documents: For PDF, author/title/etc. are cleared. Office/ODF files are unzipped, metadata XML files are removed, and comment/revision author names are blanked, then repacked.

This tool does not claim universal removal of every unknown proprietary structure. It reports the operation it actually performed.
